BRING YOUR OWN DATA

Make your CSV make sense.

Check the convention against your export. OpenAI-style totals typically include cache; Anthropic-style fresh-input fields exclude it. USD only. No currency conversion.

A clear path from CSV.

Export your text-model token usage as CSV. Meter needs a date, a model, and an input or output token count. Map your columns in the import preview.

Dates: YYYY-MM-DD, ISO timestamps with a timezone, or Unix seconds/milliseconds. Quoted fields, UTF-8 BOMs, tabs and semicolons are supported.

Generic input_tokens includes cache reads and writes. For a fresh-input export, choose Input excludes cache. Do not combine files with different cache conventions before normalising them.

cost_usd is optional. A Meter export also carries cost_basis, so estimates never become recorded charges when re-imported. Otherwise enter your own USD rates. Unpriced rows stay visible but are excluded from known cost. Duplicate rows are flagged and kept, because identical calls can be legitimate.

One file at a time, up to 10 MB / 50,000 rows / 100 models. Importing replaces the current dataset and clears its rate card. Everything stays in memory; reloading clears it. Downloads you save remain on your device.

Not an invoice reconciliation system. No automatic account connections, live pricing, subscription-quota conversion, or multimodal/tool-fee support.
